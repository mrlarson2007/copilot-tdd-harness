Copilot TDD Harness · Eval Report

Three prototype ways to present our eval results to visitors. All three read the same data file (data/eval-report.js) and compare models with the TDD harness versus no skills at all.

Sample data. The numbers in these prototypes are illustrative and were generated, not measured.

A · Scorecard

A single leaderboard: one overall score per model with and without the harness, plus the lift. Expand a row for per-metric detail.

Best for: a quick, scannable answer on a landing page.

B · Dashboard

Interactive filters by scenario and metric, a grouped bar chart, a model × metric heatmap, and a per-scenario table.

Best for: technical visitors who want to dig into the data.

C · Story

A narrative before/after page: what agents do without skills, the most common failure modes, gains per model, and the cost.

Best for: explaining why the harness exists to new visitors.
